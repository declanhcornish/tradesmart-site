    What to do next

    1. Ask about CTE at your school

      Most high schools and career centers run Career and Technical Education programs in these trades. Your counselor can get you in.

    2. Look up a registered apprenticeship

      Search real programs near you at apprenticeship.gov, run by the U.S. Department of Labor.

    3. Get matched on TradeSmart

      TradeSmart connects students with trade companies that have been checked and approved. Launching across America. Create your profile.
